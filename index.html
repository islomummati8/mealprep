Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Calorie & Diet Tracker (com.mealprep.ai), the app published on Google Play by Safe Media Official. “Mealprep” and “BiteWise” are alternate project names for this same app. It explains how the app collects, uses, stores, and shares user data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Email address and username provided during registration or Google Sign-In.
  • App Data: Meal plans, recipe preferences, and user configurations.
  • Approximate Location: We do not collect precise GPS coordinates. Approximate location (city or region level) is used exclusively to recommend region-specific dishes and recipes.
  • Media, Voice, Files & AI Interaction: While interacting with the AI assistant, users can:
    • Upload Images: For analyzing food or recipes.
    • Audio/Voice Messages: To record and send voice prompts to the AI.
    • Upload Files: To share recipes or documents with the AI.
    These files and voice records are processed temporarily to fulfill your requests.
  • Billing Data: Handled securely via Google Play Billing.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs collected via Firebase services.

2. How We Use Information

Data is used to operate core features (meal planning, AI assistant integration supporting voice, images, and files), provide region-based food recommendations, manage accounts, verify subscriptions, and improve app performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is processed securely within Firebase and AI cloud services solely for app functionality.
